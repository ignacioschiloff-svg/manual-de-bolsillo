🔢 14. Hammer pants

🎨 ¿Qué se ve en la imagen?

🧠 ¿Qué dice Sketchy y cómo interpretarlo?
→ Los "pantalones anchísimos" representan las piernas hinchadas de la elefantiasis.

📜 Texto literal de Sketchy:

Infection with Wuchereria bancrofti causes lymphatic filariasis, where adult worms live in and block lymphatic vessels, leading to impaired lymph drainage, chronic inflammation, and fibrosis, which over time results in elephantiasis, characterized by massive, disfiguring lymphedema
