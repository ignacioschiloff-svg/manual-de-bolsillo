🔢 1. Phyllis's chocolates

El letrero de la tienda "Phyllis's Chocolate Covered Cherries".

💡 ¿Qué significa clínicamente?

Haemophilus influenzae (a gram-negative coccobacillus)

🔢 15. "Sugar DIPped"

📜 Texto literal de Sketchy:

The capsular polysaccharide of Hib is a poor immunogen in infants; to enhance immunogenicity, the Hib polysaccharide is covalently coupled to diphtheria toxoid
