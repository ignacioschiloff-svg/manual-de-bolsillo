🔢 7. Patchy lower lung blueprint

🧠 ¿Qué dice Sketchy y cómo interpretarlo?
→ El "plano con forma de pulmón" representa directamente los hallazgos radiográficos pulmonares en parches.

📜 Texto literal de Sketchy:

In Legionnaires disease, a severe form of pneumonia caused by Legionella pneumophila, chest X-rays often reveal patchy lobar infiltrate that progress to consolidation, predominantly in the mid-to-lower zones
