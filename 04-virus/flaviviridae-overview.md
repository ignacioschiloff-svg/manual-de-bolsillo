🔢 3. "New FLAVORS!" Fruit Punch Stand

🎨 ¿Qué se ve en la imagen?

💡 ¿Qué significa clínicamente?

🧠 ¿Qué dice Sketchy y cómo interpretarlo?
→ "FLAVORS" (sabores) es la clave fonética directa de "FLAVIviridae", y el puesto de bebidas agrupa a todos sus miembros.

📜 Texto literal de Sketchy:

The Flaviviridae family includes viruses in the genera Flavivirus and Hepacivirus, (five clinically relevant members such as dengue virus, yellow fever virus, Zika virus, West Nile virus, and hepatitis C virus), many flaviviruses are arboviruses (ARthropod BOrne viruses) transmitted to humans by insect vectors (e.g., mosquitoes (Aedes or Culex species); in contrast, hepatitis C virus is primarily bloodborne and not vector-transmitted
